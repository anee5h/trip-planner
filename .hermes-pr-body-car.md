## Precise summary

Deterministic rental-car travel-time fallback (SafeGround car arcs) for Home/Explore discovery. Discovery remains **zero-ORS** (`#326` untouched).

## Confirmed root cause
- `/api/car-route` + ORS healthy (12/12 live calls available).
- Discovery performs zero provider calls → car-capable destinations (e.g. Shima Onsen) receive no provider evidence, and the deterministic same-zone km estimator is capped at **120 km** — Shima (~140 km road) fell outside → "Travel time unavailable".
- Hakone "worked" only because it lacks a car mode and fell through to the train data.

## Before / after
- **Before:** rental-car discovery → most car-capable destinations beyond the local radius show `Travel time unavailable`.
- **After:** Shima Onsen **~115–160 min (car, estimated)**; Tomioka **~94–120**; Fukushima **~165–215**; Sado (cross-water) **stays unavailable**; Hakone keeps its granular local estimate.

## Fallback hierarchy (unchanged precedence, new rung)
```
Provider-capable surfaces:  provider route → SafeGround km → car zone arc → unavailable
Zero-provider discovery:     SafeGround km → car zone arc → unavailable
```
Mode provenance is strict: `getGroundRoute(…, "car")` only — a train/shinkansen arc can never satisfy a car request (regression-tested).

## What was added
- **77 car arcs** (`ground-routes.json`, mode `car`, round conservative ranges) across the **5 supported origin zones** (tokyo, kanagawa, osaka, hiroshima, fukuoka) → 43 zones/subzones. Every arc carries `sourceUrl` + `authority` + a `basis` note describing the corridor derivation (NEXCO East driveplaza / NEXCO Central drive-info / METEX / JB Honshi pages — operator-standard travel times, no implied live-traffic precision).
- **Gunma road subzones** (south / northwest / northeast) via a municipality-prefix map; muni-less Gunma records (e.g. Oze NP) default to the south key. (Smoke caught Shima's real municipality = **Nakanojo** — added to the northwest map.)
- `getCarZoneArcEstimate` in `TripDurationService` — deterministic origin-key resolution by nearest supported-origin centroid (≤ 40 km; no catalogue-loader dependency — identical in browser/tsx/CI).
- Island guard: ferry-only island zones (sado, oki, iki…) stay unavailable even when the prefecture arc exists; `awaji` (fixed Akashi Kaikyo link) excepted. `localAccessUnestimated === true` destinations are respected (parity with the km estimator).
- **Corrected coverage audit** `scripts/audit/rental-car-coverage-2026.ts` + **9 regression tests**.
