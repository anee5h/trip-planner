### 5. Root Cause Resolution: 129 vs 159 Destinations Filter Issue

- **Root Cause Diagnosis**: The 30 new destinations had `budgetMin` and `budgetMax` defined but lacked `budgetRecommended`. In [`BudgetService.ts`](file:///home/aneesh/Desktop/projects/trip/src/shared/services/budget/BudgetService.ts), `dest.budgetRecommended - 3000` evaluated to `NaN`, causing `NaN <= 100000` budget filter checks to fail and excluding those 30 destinations.
- **Data Fix**: Populated `budgetRecommended = Math.round((budgetMin + budgetMax) / 2)` across all 30 new destinations in `destinations-index.json` and `public/data/destinations/*.json`.
- **Defensive Code Hardening**: Added fallback `recBudget = dest.budgetRecommended || dest.budgetMin || 5000` in [`BudgetService.ts`](file:///home/aneesh/Desktop/projects/trip/src/shared/services/budget/BudgetService.ts) to permanently prevent `NaN` calculations.
- **Result**: Verified `/destinations` now displays **159 destinations matching** (100% of dataset).
