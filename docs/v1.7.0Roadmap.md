I actually agree with **about 80%** of Gemini's review. The key is separating **real usability issues** from **stylistic preferences**.

I **strongly agree** with:

- Drawer bleed/overlay (this should be fixed)
- Horizontal clipping and scroll affordance
- Tightening spacing
- Better mobile navigation
- Floating CTA positioning
- Consistency improvements

I **partially agree** with:

- Too much Tailwind look (yes, but your branding already differentiates it)
- Pill overuse (worth reducing)

I **don't fully agree** with:

- "Looks AI generated because it uses rounded cards." Most modern consumer apps (Linear, Arc, Airbnb, Notion Calendar, etc.) also use rounded cards. The problem isn't the rounded corners—it's repetition and lack of hierarchy.

## Final recommendation

I would **avoid adding major functionality in v1.7.0**. From the screenshots, TabiMap already has a rich feature set: discovery, planning, passport, collections, achievements, badges, statistics, and personalized recommendations. The next release should make those existing features feel more refined and cohesive.

A polished v1.7.0 will likely do more for users' perception of quality than another large feature drop.
