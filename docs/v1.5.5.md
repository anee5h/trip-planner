I think v1.5.5 is the right time to make **Passport feel like a product**, not just a dashboard.

I would **not** remove Achievements or Badges. Instead, I'd clearly define what each represents and make them work together.

---

# Architecture & Implementation Plan: v1.5.5 — Passport Identity & Badge System

Transform Passport from a collection of progress cards into a personal travel identity by redefining the responsibilities of **Achievements**, **Badges**, and **Collections**, while preserving existing navigation.

---

# Goal

Establish clear ownership for each travel system.

| Feature          | Navigation | Purpose                                               |
| ---------------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Collections**  | Discover   | Browse and complete curated travel lists              |
| **Achievements** | Passport   | Celebrate major travel milestones                     |
| **Badges**       | Passport   | Represent travel identity, interests, and specialties |
| **Timeline**     | Passport   | Personal travel history                               |
| **Statistics**   | Passport   | Travel analytics                                      |

---

# Design Principles

## Collections (No Changes)

Collections remain the exploration engine.

Users:

- discover lists
- plan trips
- track completion
- browse official collections

Examples:

- UNESCO World Heritage
- Original 12 Castles
- National Parks
- Three Great Gardens

Collections remain the **only** place where collection completion is managed.

---

## Achievements

Achievements celebrate significant accomplishments.

Instead of acting as another collection browser, achievements become **milestones derived from travel progress**.

Examples:

| Achievement           | Unlock Condition                        |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| First Journey         | Visit first destination                 |
| Castle Master         | Complete Original 12 Castles collection |
| Heritage Guardian     | Complete UNESCO Japan collection        |
| All Regions Explored  | Visit every Japanese region             |
| Four Seasons Traveler | Travel during all four seasons          |
| Japan Complete        | Visit all 47 prefectures                |

Achievements are:

- permanent
- milestone-based
- unlocked once
- chronological

---

## Badges

Badges represent **who the traveler is**, not what collections they've completed.

Inspired by:

- Japanese souvenir pins
- Tourism campaign emblems
- Regional commemorative medals
- Illustrated travel badges

Badges do **not** display progress bars.

# Passport Event Flow

All Passport systems respond to travel events.

```text
Visit Destination

        │
        ▼

Update Visit History

        │
        ├────────► Collections Progress
        │
        ├────────► Achievement Engine
        │
        ├────────► Badge Engine
        │
        ▼

Refresh Passport
```

Collections remain independent but become one of several inputs into Passport.

# Acceptance Criteria

### Collections

- [ ] Continue to manage curated travel lists and completion progress.
- [ ] Remain the primary location for collection tracking.

### Achievements

- [ ] Represent milestone accomplishments rather than collection progress.
- [ ] Unlock once and remain permanently earned.
- [ ] Display unlock dates and descriptions.
- [ ] Remove duplicate collection-style progress cards where practical.

### Badges

- [ ] Feature approximately 20–25 handcrafted badges.
- [ ] Use premium illustrated circular designs inspired by Japanese travel memorabilia.
- [ ] Represent travel identity rather than collection completion.
- [ ] Display unlocked/locked state without progress bars.
- [ ] Include badge artwork, description, unlock criteria, and earned date.

### Passport

- [ ] Each Passport tab has a distinct purpose.
- [ ] No duplicate progress tracking between Passport and Discover.
- [ ] Badge and Achievement designs are visually distinct.
- [ ] Passport feels like a personal travel journal rather than a second collections browser.

---

## Vision

The goal is to make Passport feel like something travelers want to revisit after every trip:

- **Collections** answer: _"What should I explore next?"_
- **Achievements** answer: _"What milestones have I reached?"_
- **Badges** answer: _"What kind of traveler have I become?"_

That separation gives every feature a clear role while making Passport feel more personal and memorable without changing your existing navigation model.
