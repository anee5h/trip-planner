## Semantics notes

**Destinations reachable ONLY by limited express (surcharge mandatory):**

- Nikko / Kinugawa Onsen via Tobu: base-fare-only trips are possible on local/rapid Tobu trains, but the practical day-trip service is LEX Kegon/Revaty (all-reserved). LEX surcharge is effectively mandatory for a same-day visit (Tobu base ¥1,400 + ¥1,650).
- Hakone-Yumoto via Odakyu: Romancecar optional but strongly preferred (75 min vs ~110–120 min local via Odawara); base-only is possible but slow.
- Takayama (Hida), Yufuin (Yufuin no Mori), Beppu (Sonic), Noboribetsu/Hakodate (Suzuran/Hokuto): LEX is effectively the only practical service on these corridors — **base-only "train" claims would understate real cost**.
- Kofu/Kawaguchiko: Kaiji LEX to Otsuki optional; local Chuo trains exist but are slow (2h30+). Fujikyu Otsuki–Kawaguchiko base verified (¥1,170).

**"Train" corridors that actually mix JR + private operators (single corridor, two operators):**

- Tokyo–Nikko/Kinugawa: JR Utsunomiya/Nikko lines (slower, no LEX) vs Tobu (LEX, faster) — fare and time differ by operator.
- Tokyo–Hakone: Odakyu (Romancecar) vs JR Tokaido to Odawara + Hakone Tozan (two operators).
- Tokyo–Kawaguchiko: JR Chuo (Kaiji) + Fujikyuko (two operators, two tickets).
- Osaka–Nara: JR Yamatoji vs Kintetsu (LEX).
- Osaka–Kobe: JR vs Hankyu (vs Hanshin) — three parallel operators.
- Osaka–Wakayama: JR (Hanwa/Kuroshio) vs Nankai (Southern) — different stations (Tennoji vs Namba).
- Nagoya–Toyohashi: JR Tokaido vs Meitetsu (parallel).
- Nagoya–Irago: JR/Meitetsu to Toyohashi + Toyotetsu Atsumi Line + Toyotetsu bus (three legs, rail+bus).
- Hakata–Nagasaki: post-2022 it is Relay Kamome (conventional) + Shinkansen Kamome (two-train relay) — a conventional-only fare no longer exists.

**Corridors where "train" estimate must NOT be offered / excluded:**

- Hakata–Nagasaki: conventional through-service discontinued 2022-09-23 → row is EXCLUDED from conventional ledger; only shinkansen relay exists.
- Hakata–Kagoshima: primary service is Kyushu Shinkansen; conventional Kagoshima Main Line is a slow non-tourist service — EXCLUDED from conventional claims.
- Nagoya–Irago: train+bus mix, single-rail fare unverified → EXCLUDED (bus leg dominates final km).
- Hiroshima Airport: rail does not reach airport; bus is primary (¥1,500/50 min) → EXCLUDED from rail ledger, noted for completeness.
- Fukuoka/Nagoya/Sendai airports: served by subway/Meitetsu/Access Line respectively — included for completeness with clear operator labels.

**Verification gaps to close before production (all ⚠️/derived rows):**
