## Data-model decisions

The audit distinguishes three levels:

1. **UNESCO property:** the inscribed serial, cultural, or natural property, for example `Historic Monuments of Ancient Kyoto` or `Sado Island Gold Mines`.
2. **Official component:** a named component of a serial property, for example Ujigami Shrine, Motsu-ji, the Koyasan sites, or the Horyu-ji-area monuments.
3. **Meguruto destination / visitor POI:** a useful catalogue record that represents a property, a component, or a visitor-accessible component area. A visitor POI is not silently promoted to the whole property.

Collection membership is the grouping mechanism for serial properties. `parentDestinationId` remains municipal containment only. Remote or cross-municipality access is represented with `gatewayHubId`.

## False-positive and programme audit

- **Sengan-en Garden:** removed the `UNESCO` tag and the claim that Sengan-en itself is a component of Sites of Japan's Meiji Industrial Revolution. The bilingual description now identifies the nearby Shoko Shuseikan industrial complex as the UNESCO-linked context. Sengan-en is not in `unesco-japan`.
- **Takachiho Kagura:** removed `UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage` from the destination highlights. It is not a UNESCO World Heritage property and must not be conflated with the World Heritage collection. The destination remains a valid cultural-performance POI.
- **Namahage Museum:** its intangible-heritage wording remains outside `unesco-japan`; no World Heritage membership was added.
- **Sakai City Museum & Daisen Kofun View:** removed the `UNESCO` category. The museum is a visitor/context record beside the Mozu-Furuichi property, not a second UNESCO component record.
- **Kyoto city and Nara city hubs:** not added to the UNESCO collection. Property membership stays on specific monuments/components.
- **Yakushima Town:** its hub description now says it is a gateway to the World Heritage wilderness; the municipality itself is not a collection member.
- **`kyoto-historic`**: retained as a broad visitor overview without adding a duplicate property-level collection member; unlike the existing property-level `nara-historic` record, its component records carry the UNESCO membership.
- **Ujigami Shrine and Motsu-ji:** added because both are existing, useful, bilingual destination records for official UNESCO components. This is catalogue completion, not speculative expansion.
- **Passport achievement trigger:** updated `heritage-guardian` to the canonical `unesco-japan` collection ID so completing the audited collection can satisfy the achievement. No legacy UNESCO collection ID remains in the achievement trigger.
- No Intangible Cultural Heritage, Global Geopark, Biosphere Reserve, or Memory of the World record was added to the World Heritage collection.
